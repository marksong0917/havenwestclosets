Mudroom & Built-In Questions

What can a mudroom or built-in include?

Bench seating with lift-up or drawer storage underneath, ventilated shoe cubbies and deep drawers for winter boots, solid-mounted coat hook walls, and hard-wearing, scuff-resistant finishes that are easy to wipe clean.

How does the process work?

Consultation first: we measure your entryway and design around how your household comes and goes, build in our Concord shop, and install with our own crew.

Is it built to fit my space?

Yes — every unit is built in our Concord shop to your space's exact dimensions, never fixed-size modules.

Is the work guaranteed?

Yes — installed by our own crew and backed by a lifetime workmanship warranty.
