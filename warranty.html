Lifetime Workmanship Warranty

Every closet we build is backed by a lifetime workmanship warranty. If a panel delaminates, warps, chips, or otherwise fails due to a manufacturing or installation defect, we will repair or replace it at no cost to you, for as long as you own the property.

Materials

The boxes, doors, drawers, and panels we build with are covered under the lifetime workmanship warranty above. We build using composite wood panels sourced from established North American manufacturers — Uniboard, Tafisa Canada, and EGGER — certified to FSC® responsible-forestry standards and meeting TSCA Title VI / CARB Phase 2 formaldehyde emission requirements.

Hardware

Moving hardware — hinges, drawer slides, and hanging rods — is covered for 5 years from the date of installation. This is a separate term from the lifetime workmanship coverage above, since hardware is mechanical and wears with regular use.

What's Not Covered

  • Normal wear and tear, including wear on high-use hardware
  • Damage caused by improper use, accidents, or modifications made after installation
  • Products or materials supplied by a third party, not installed by us

Making a Warranty Claim

Contact us at info@harbourclosets.com or (905) 660-6696 with your name, address, and a description or photo of the issue. We'll respond within 24–48 business hours.

General Terms

This warranty applies only to work completed by Harbour Closets and is not transferable to a subsequent owner of the property unless otherwise agreed in writing.
