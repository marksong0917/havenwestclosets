Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

The Short Version

We collect only the details you submit through our contact form or phone calls to prepare quotes and schedule closet consultations. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

What We Collect & Why

When you use our contact form or call us, we collect your name, email, phone number, and any project details you choose to share. We use this only to respond to your inquiry, prepare an estimate, and schedule a consultation.

Third-Party Services

Our contact form is delivered through FormSubmit, a third-party form service, which emails your submission to us. The information you enter in the form passes through that service only to deliver your message. Our website is hosted by a third-party web host, which may log standard technical data (such as IP address) as part of normal server operation.

How Long We Keep It

We keep inquiry details for as long as needed to respond to you and, if a project moves forward, for the duration of that project and standard record-keeping afterward.

Access & Deletion

You can ask what information we have about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing marksong0000@gmail.com.

This page is a plain-language summary and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Before relying on it for compliance purposes, have it reviewed by a licensed Ontario lawyer familiar with Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA).
