Reach-In Closet Questions

What does the reach-in starting price include?

The 6-ft reach-in starting configuration — from $1,930 — includes delivery and installation, and HST is included. Prices are estimates; the final price is confirmed in a written quote after the site measure.

How does the design & site measure work?

The in-person design & site measure is $200 + HST, paid by an emailed payment link. The fee is non-refundable, includes three rounds of drawing revisions (further rounds are $200 + HST per additional three rounds), and is credited toward your order up to $400 when you order within 90 days of paying the fee.

What can a reach-in include?

Full-height hinged doors with soft-close hinges, lit glass display cabinets, custom drawer towers sized to your wardrobe, and double-hang sections that double your capacity in the same footprint. Every unit is built to your wall's exact dimensions — never fixed-size modules.

How long does it take, and is the work guaranteed?

We measure your wall and design around it, build in our Concord shop, and install with our own crew. Most projects take a few weeks, confirmed in writing. The work is backed by a lifetime workmanship warranty.
