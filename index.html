About Harbour Closets

We only build closets. That's exactly the point.

Most cabinetry shops split their attention across kitchens, vanities, offices, and everything in between. We don't. Every hour in our Ontario shop goes into one thing: closets built to fit the exact shape of your space and how you actually use it.

From a single reach-in to a full walk-in dressing room, every project is custom-built to order — measured, cut, and assembled here in Ontario, then installed by our own team. Nothing off-the-shelf, nothing forced to fit.

Questions

A few things people ask

How long does a custom closet take?

Most projects are ready 2–6 weeks after your design is finalized, depending on complexity and material availability.

Do you build reach-in closets, or only walk-ins?

Both, and everything in between. From a single reach-in closet to a full walk-in dressing room, every project is custom-built to order.

Where are your closets actually made?

In our own shop in Ontario. Every piece is measured, cut, and assembled here, then installed by our own team — nothing outsourced.

What areas do you serve?

Homeowners across Concord, Vaughan, Toronto, Richmond Hill, Markham, and the rest of the GTA.

Is there a warranty?

Yes — every project is backed by a lifetime workmanship warranty. See our Warranty & Terms page for full details.
