QUESTIONS

Quick Answers

How much does a custom closet cost?

Every project is quoted individually. Consultations are free, and after an in-home measurement you get a written quote — no obligation, and no price is final until it's in writing.

How custom is it, really?

Fully — every unit is built in our Concord shop to your room's exact dimensions, not assembled from fixed-size modules.

Is the work guaranteed?

Yes. Havenwest closets carry a lifetime warranty for as long as you own your home. Full terms are on our warranty page.

How long does it take, and how do we start?

Book a free consultation, we measure and design, build in our Concord shop, then install. Most projects follow that path in a few weeks depending on size — and we'll give you a timeline in writing before we start.
