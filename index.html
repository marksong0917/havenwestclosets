About Havenwest

Havenwest Closets & Storage Solutions designs and builds luxury custom closets in Concord, Ontario. Every closet is measured and built around your space — walk-ins, reach-ins, wardrobes, and mudrooms — and our workmanship is backed by a lifetime warranty. We serve homeowners across the Greater Toronto Area.

Questions

Quick Answers

How much does a custom closet cost?

Every project is quoted individually. The in-home design & measurement is $200 + HST, paid by an emailed payment link. The fee is non-refundable, includes three rounds of drawing revisions (further rounds are $200 + HST per additional three rounds), and is credited toward your order up to $400 when you order within 90 days of paying the fee. After the measurement you get a written quote, no obligation. Prices shown are estimates; the final price is confirmed in a written quote after the site measure.

How custom is it, really?

Fully. Every unit is built in our Concord shop to your room's exact dimensions — never fixed-size modules.

Is the work guaranteed?

Yes — a lifetime workmanship warranty for as long as you own your home. Full terms are on our warranty page.

How long does it take, and how do we start?

Book a consultation; we measure and design, build in Concord, then install. Most projects take a few weeks, confirmed in writing.

What do you build?

Custom walk-in closets, reach-in closets & wardrobes, and mudrooms & built-ins — measured and built around your space, so no inch goes to waste.

Where do you serve?

We design and build in Concord, Ontario and serve homeowners across the Greater Toronto Area.
