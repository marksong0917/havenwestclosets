How much does a custom closet cost?

Every project is quoted individually. Consultations are free — after an in-home measurement you get a written quote, no obligation.

Fully. Every unit is built in our Concord shop to your room's exact dimensions — never fixed-size modules.

Is the work guaranteed?

Yes — a lifetime warranty for as long as you own your home. Full terms are on our warranty page.

How long does it take, and how do we start?

Book a free consultation; we measure and design, build in Concord, then install. Most projects take a few weeks, confirmed in writing.
